The boundary

Fizz Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Cylinder yields, bottle counts and exchange prices are labeled norms, not measurements of your machine, your water or your store - real yield depends on how cold the water is, how hard you press and how you store the bottle. Follow the machine's manual over anything this page says, and never carbonate anything but plain water.

The sixty liters on the box are counted at the gentlest press. Every extra push of the button spends the cylinder faster, so the strong fizz everyone actually wants pours a fraction of the label. Fizz Math does the honest arithmetic between the cylinder, the bottle and the receipt.

Three panels. The fizz truth translates the label by fizz level. The bottle truth counts the bottles a cylinder fills. The cost truth weighs the exchange against the store's liter price.
